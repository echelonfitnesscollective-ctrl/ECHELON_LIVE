ON-SITE GROUP FITNESS FOR YOUR COMMUNITY

A free, professionally coached weekly group fitness class — on your property, every week — with zero cost passed on to residents and zero operational lift for your team. Echelon brings the coach, the programming, and the energy. You bring the space.

HOW IT WORKS

  • A certified Echelon coach travels to your property on a fixed weekly day and time — clubhouse, gym room, or courtyard, whatever space you have.
  • One standing 45-minute group session, open to all residents, no sign-up friction and no per-class fee for them to pay.
  • Fully programmed and led — your team doesn't manage equipment, scheduling, or instruction.
  • Consistent, reliable scheduling with a written makeup policy if a session is ever missed.

WHY IT WORKS FOR YOUR PROPERTY

  • A marketable amenity — "free weekly fitness classes" is a differentiator in listings, tours, and renewal conversations.
  • Supports resident retention and satisfaction without adding to your operating burden.
  • No equipment, staffing, or liability of running it yourself — Echelon owns the delivery.
  • Backed by an established local training brand already running structured programs for individuals and private groups.
