# TEMPLATE: DELIVERING A PERSONALIZED PLAN TO A MEMBER

Use this when handing a member their personalized version of one of the 6 base 12-week programs (1-on-1 Coaching or 12-Week Transformation clients). This is a conversation checklist, not a script to read verbatim, the goal is the member leaves confident and clear, not talked at.

---

## 1. Before the Conversation

- [ ] Base model selected and personalized (exercise substitutions made for equipment/injury history, load starting points set from intake data)
- [ ] Plan seeded into the system (Workout Library → Program Templates → assigned via Today's Work, or the first week at minimum)
- [ ] Any modification flags from intake (pain history, pregnancy, joint concerns) cross-checked against the final plan, confirm nothing in Week 1 conflicts with a known limitation

## 2. What to Say (talking points, not a script)

1. **Confirm the goal in their words first.** "Last time we talked, you said the goal was ___, has anything changed since then?" Personalization starts with re-confirming the goal, not assuming last conversation still holds.
2. **Explain the shape of the plan, not just the exercises.** Walk them through the split (which days hit what), the 12-week arc (what Phase 1 vs. Phase 3 feels like), and where the deload/reassessment week lands. Members who understand *why* the plan is structured this way stick with it longer than members who are just handed a list of exercises.
3. **Walk one full day together.** Pick Day 1 and go through it exercise by exercise: what it targets, the form cue they should hold onto, and their modification if one applies to them specifically.
4. **Set the check-in expectation.** Confirm how and how often you'll check in (weekly via the Coaching Hub message thread, Today's Work status, or however you both prefer) and what you want from them (adherence honesty over "everything's perfect" answers).
5. **Ask them to repeat it back.** "What's your plan for this week?", catching a misunderstanding here costs a minute; catching it after a missed or wrong week costs more.

## 3. What to Confirm They Understand

- [ ] Where to find their plan (Today's Work panel in the Coaching Hub)
- [ ] How to read an exercise card (sets/reps, targets, cues, and where modifications live)
- [ ] What to do if something hurts (stop, message you, don't push through, and see a clinician if it persists)
- [ ] How to mark a day complete
- [ ] That the plan is a living document, it gets adjusted based on how they respond, not treated as fixed for 12 weeks no matter what

## 4. First Check-In (within the first week)

- [ ] Did the first 1–2 sessions actually happen?
- [ ] Any exercise that felt wrong, too easy, or confusing?
- [ ] Any modification that wasn't accounted for?
- [ ] Energy/soreness reasonable, or a red flag worth addressing early?

Catching friction in week one prevents a member quietly disengaging by week three. A short, low-effort check-in early is worth more than an elaborate one late.
