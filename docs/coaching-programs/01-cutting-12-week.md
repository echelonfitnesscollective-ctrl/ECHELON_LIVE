# ECHELON 12-WEEK BASE MODEL: CUTTING

**Goal:** Fat loss while preserving lean muscle and strength.
**Audience:** Intermediate+ clients with a training base, cleared through intake (PAR-Q/health history), no unmanaged pain flags.
**Format:** 4 lifting days/week + 2 conditioning finishers + 1 active recovery day. Deload built into Week 12.
**Status:** Base model, personalize load, exercise substitutions, and cardio volume per client before assigning. Seed into `exercise_library` → `workouts` → `program_templates` once approved.

---

## 1. Weekly Split

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Upper Body Strength |
| 2 | Lower Body Strength |
| 3 | Active Recovery (mobility, walk, optional light cardio) |
| 4 | Full-Body Metabolic Conditioning |
| 5 | Posterior Chain + Core |
| 6 | Conditioning Finisher (15–20 min, intervals or circuit) |
| 7 | Rest |

---

## 2. 12-Week Periodization

| Phase | Weeks | Rep Range | Rest | Intensity (RPE) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1: Foundation | 1–4 | 10–12 | 60s | 6–7 | Strength retention, movement quality, build work capacity |
| 2: Density | 5–8 | 8–10 | 45s | 7–8 | Reduce rest as sets hit top of range; metabolic stress rises |
| 3: Peak Push | 9–11 | 6–8 | 30–45s | 8–9 | Heavier loads, added finishers, highest weekly output |
| Deload | 12 | 10–12 | 60s | 5–6 | Volume cut ~40%, reassess measurements/photos, plan next block |

**Progression rule (density method):** once a client hits the top of the rep range for all working sets two sessions in a row, cut rest by 10–15 seconds before adding load. This keeps sessions metabolically demanding as strength improves, which is the driver for a cutting phase (versus a straight linear-load progression, which is better suited to Bulking/Muscle below).

## 3. Day 1: Upper Body Strength

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 x 8–12 | 60–90s | Chest, Triceps, Front Delts |
| Chest-Supported Row | 4 x 8–12 | 60–90s | Lats, Rhomboids, Rear Delts |
| Seated Overhead Press | 3 x 8–10 | 60s | Shoulders, Triceps |
| Lat Pulldown | 3 x 10–12 | 45–60s | Lats, Biceps |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 x 10–12 | 45–60s | Upper Chest |
| Cable Face Pull | 3 x 15 | 30–45s | Rear Delts, Rotator Cuff |
| Standing Cable Curl | 2 x 12–15 | 30s | Biceps |
| Rope Tricep Pushdown | 2 x 12–15 | 30s | Triceps |

**Form cues (Bench Press):** Feet flat, shoulder blades pinned and down, bar path touches low chest, drive floor through feet.
**Coaching cues:** "Pull the bar apart" for lat engagement on rows; "Rip the floor" for bench leg drive.
**Modification up:** Add a 2-second pause at the chest on bench; tempo eccentrics (3-1-1) on rows.
**Modification down:** Machine chest press and machine row in place of free-weight versions; reduce ROM on overhead press if shoulder impingement present.
**Modification (pregnancy):** Replace flat bench with incline press to reduce supine positioning after first trimester; avoid unsupported standing overhead press past week 20, use seated back-supported version; cap RPE at 6.

---

## 4. Day 2: Lower Body Strength

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back Squat (or Goblet Squat regression) | 4 x 8–12 | 90s | Quads, Glutes |
| Romanian Deadlift | 4 x 8–12 | 90s | Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back |
| Walking Lunge | 3 x 10/leg | 60s | Quads, Glutes, Balance |
| Leg Press | 3 x 10–12 | 60s | Quads, Glutes |
| Seated Leg Curl | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Hamstrings |
| Standing Calf Raise | 3 x 15–20 | 30s | Calves |
| Hanging Knee Raise | 3 x 12–15 | 30s | Core |

**Form cues (Squat):** Brace before descent, knees track over toes, hips and chest rise together out of the hole.
**Coaching cues:** "Spread the floor" on squats and RDLs for hip/knee tracking; "Hinge, don't bend" on RDL setup.
**Modification up:** Add pause squats (2s at depth); tempo RDLs (4s eccentric).
**Modification down:** Box squat to a target height for depth confidence; trap-bar deadlift instead of RDL if lower-back irritation is present.
**Modification (pregnancy):** Goblet squat instead of back-loaded squat once balance/center of gravity shifts; avoid supine leg curl machines that require lying flat past first trimester, sub standing or seated cable curl; skip hanging knee raise, sub standing pallof press for core.

---

## 5. Day 4: Full-Body Metabolic Conditioning

Circuit format, 3–4 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 90s rest between rounds.

| Exercise | Reps/Time | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| Kettlebell Swing | 15 | Posterior Chain, Conditioning |
| Push-Up (or incline push-up) | 12–15 | Chest, Triceps, Core |
| Goblet Squat | 12 | Quads, Glutes |
| Bent-Over Dumbbell Row | 12/side | Back |
| Mountain Climbers | 30s | Core, Conditioning |
| Plank Hold | 30–45s | Core |

**Coaching cues:** Cap heart rate climb by regulating tempo, not skipping reps, "smooth and controlled beats fast and sloppy" in a metabolic circuit.
**Modification down:** Reduce rounds to 2–3, or extend rest to 2 min between rounds; swap kettlebell swing for banded hip hinge if lower back is a limiter.
**Modification (pregnancy):** Remove kettlebell swing and mountain climbers (high-impact/high-intra-abdominal-pressure); replace with step-ups and standing bicycle crunch at controlled tempo; keep heart rate conversational.

---

## 6. Day 5: Posterior Chain + Core

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deadlift (conventional or trap-bar) | 4 x 6–8 | 90–120s | Full Posterior Chain |
| Hip Thrust | 3 x 10–12 | 60s | Glutes |
| Single-Leg RDL | 3 x 10/leg | 60s | Hamstrings, Balance |
| Cable Pull-Through | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Glutes, Hamstrings |
| Pallof Press | 3 x 12/side | 30s | Anti-Rotation Core |
| Cable Woodchop | 3 x 12/side | 30s | Rotational Core |

**Form cues (Deadlift):** Bar over mid-foot, lats engaged before the pull, hips and shoulders rise at the same rate.
**Modification down:** Trap-bar over conventional barbell for a more upright torso and lower lower-back shear; rack-pull from knee height if hinge mechanics need more coaching reps first.
**Modification (pregnancy):** Cap deadlift load conservatively and stop conventional-stance deadlifting once belly interferes with bar path, sub sumo-stance or cable pull-through only; avoid loaded rotational core work (woodchop) after first trimester, sub bird-dog.

---

## 7. Conditioning Finisher (Day 6, 15–20 min)

Rotate weekly between:
- **Intervals:** 30s on / 30s off, bike or row, 10–14 rounds.
- **Circuit:** 5 bodyweight movements, 40s work / 20s rest, 4 rounds.

Cap intensity at RPE 7–8 in Phase 1–2; RPE 8–9 permitted in Phase 3. Always optional to swap for a 30–40 min brisk walk if a client is running high fatigue that week, flag in the weekly check-in rather than pushing through.

---

## 8. Coach Notes

- **Weekly check-in:** weight trend (not single-day), waist measurement, 1–2 progress photos, energy/sleep/soreness self-rating 1–5, adherence to conditioning days.
- **Deload triggers (pull forward from Week 12 if needed):** two consecutive weeks of rising resting heart rate + falling sleep quality + missed sessions is a signal to deload early, not push through.
- **Personalization starting points:** swap exercises for equipment access or injury history, adjust weekly conditioning volume up/down based on recovery signals, and always confirm nutrition is supporting the training load before adding more conditioning volume, more cardio is not the first lever to pull if fat loss stalls.
- Every modification above is a **starting suggestion**, not a substitute for screening a specific client's mobility, injury history, and (where relevant) clearance from their own clinician for pregnancy or a flagged medical condition. Pain during any exercise is coached as "stop, and see a clinician", never worked through or diagnosed on the spot.
