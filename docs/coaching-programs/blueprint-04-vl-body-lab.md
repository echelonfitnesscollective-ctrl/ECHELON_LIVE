# TRAINING CARD BLUEPRINT: VL BODY LAB

**Card copy (live on site):** "A high-energy athletic training experience blending strength, conditioning, and explosive movement for members who want to build more power." Focus: strength, speed, athletic conditioning, total-body capacity. 50-minute group performance session, scales to different experience levels.
**Format type:** Recurring high-intensity group class, the group-format sibling of the individualized Performance 12-week base model; same athletic-development philosophy, delivered live to a room instead of on a personalized plan.

---

## 1. Session Shape (50 min)

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Dynamic Warm-Up + Movement Prep | 8 min | Raise heart rate, prime landing/sprint/power mechanics before any explosive work |
| Power/Speed Block | 12–15 min | Jumps, sprints, med-ball throws, or agility work, lowest-fatigue, highest-skill-demand block, always placed early while fresh |
| Strength Block | 15–18 min | Compound strength work at a group-appropriate load/format (often circuit-style for group logistics) |
| Conditioning Finisher | 8–10 min | Higher-intensity interval or circuit to close |
| Cool-Down | 3–5 min | Brief stretch, quick group recap |

## 2. Weekly Rotation (4-week cycle)

| Week | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lower-Body Power (jumps, sprints) + Lower Strength |
| 2 | Upper-Body Power (throws) + Upper Strength |
| 3 | Agility/Change-of-Direction + Total-Body Conditioning |
| 4 | Mixed Format / Team Challenge |

## 3. Scaling Design Principle

The card copy promises this "scales to different experience levels", that promise is delivered through the Power/Speed block specifically, since it's the highest-injury-risk block if scaled wrong:
- Every jump/sprint/throw drill needs a demonstrated lower-impact regression (step-up instead of box jump, standing throw instead of running throw, shuffle instead of full sprint) called out before the block starts, same as every other card's live-modification standard.
- Strength and conditioning blocks scale more simply through load and work:rest ratio, the coaching challenge in this format is specifically the power block, so give it the most attention in pre-class planning.

## 4. Live Coaching Cues & Modification Delivery

- "Quality over quantity" on every power rep, same coaching philosophy as the individualized Performance base model, the moment landing or sprint mechanics degrade for a member, cue them to that day's regression rather than pushing through fatigued reps.
- Walk the power block especially closely, this is the block where a form breakdown has the highest injury cost of anything in the group-format lineup.
- Any pain, and especially any joint or landing-related pain, is coached as "stop immediately, and see a clinician", do not let a member push through a bad landing or a joint twinge to keep pace with the group.

## 5. Coach Notes

- This card draws members who also want individualized programming, it's a natural upsell conversation into 1-on-1 Coaching or 12-Week Transformation (Performance track) for anyone who wants more than the group format can individualize.
- Because this is the highest-intensity group card, screen new members a little more carefully at sign-up than for Group Fitness or Faith & Favor, a brief injury-history check-in before their first class is worth the extra minute.
- Launch standard (per live site copy): opens once coaching coverage and a sustainable recurring schedule are confirmed, this blueprint is ready ahead of that launch decision.
