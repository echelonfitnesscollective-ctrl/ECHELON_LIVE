# TRAINING CARD BLUEPRINT: GROUP FITNESS

**Card copy (live on site):** "Train with the Collective through coached group sessions built for consistency, strength, and real community." $23 drop-in / $89/month unlimited.
**Format type:** Recurring class, not an individualized 12-week plan, this blueprint documents session structure, not day-by-day programming.

---

## 1. Session Shape (45–50 min)

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up | 5–7 min | Raise heart rate, dynamic mobility, prime the day's main movement patterns |
| Main Block | 25–30 min | The day's training focus (rotates, see weekly rotation below) |
| Finisher | 5–8 min | Short conditioning or core burst to close strong |
| Cool-Down | 3–5 min | Breathing, static stretch, quick group check-out |

## 2. Weekly Rotation (repeats on a 4-week cycle so regulars see variety without unpredictability)

| Week | Class Theme |
|---|---|
| 1 | Full-Body Strength Circuit |
| 2 | Metabolic Conditioning (higher heart rate, lighter load) |
| 3 | Strength + Core Focus |
| 4 | Mixed Format / Community Challenge (partner work, friendly competition) |

**Weight-loss track consideration:** Group Fitness is often a member's entry point into fat-loss-focused training without a personalized plan. Every week's format should include an accessible on-ramp (see Scaling below) so a member working toward weight loss gets real conditioning stimulus at their level, not just the most advanced version of the class. Coaches should default cueing toward sustainable pace and full-range movement over max output, the same coaching philosophy as the Weight Loss 12-week base model, just delivered live instead of on paper.

## 3. Group-Size Considerations

- Coach-to-member ratio should stay low enough to give real-time form correction, beyond roughly 12–15 members, plan for a coaching assistant or split the room into stations so no one trains unsupervised on a compound movement.
- Every exercise in the main block needs a demonstrated scale-up and scale-down on the spot, group classes cannot rely on individualized programming, so the coach's live cueing is what personalizes the session in the moment.

## 4. Live Coaching Cues & Modification Delivery

Because there's no written plan a member takes home, modifications are delivered verbally and by demonstration, in real time:
- State the standard version, then immediately state the "if that's too much" and "if you want more" options before the clock starts, never make a member ask.
- Walk the room during the main block, not just the warm-up, spot-correct form loudly enough for the room to hear (turns one correction into a cue for everyone).
- For any member who flags pain (not just fatigue), stop that movement for them individually and offer a modification or a rest, never coach through reported pain, and recommend seeing a clinician if it persists.

## 5. Coach Notes

- New members should be greeted before class and given a 60-second "here's how today's format works" so they're not lost in a room of regulars.
- Rotate who leads the finisher block among coaches to keep energy fresh.
- Track rough attendance trends by week/theme, if a theme consistently underperforms in attendance, that's a signal to revise it, not just repeat it hoping engagement improves.
