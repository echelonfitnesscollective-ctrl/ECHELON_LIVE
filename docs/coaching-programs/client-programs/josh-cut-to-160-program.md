## Nutrition Protocol

You told me the real gap already: you've been to the gym and seen progress, but never had a plan. The same is almost certainly true for your eating, so here's the structure. Exact gram targets lock in once you log your starting weight in the Hub Week 1, here's what Week 1 runs on.

| Macro | Target (locks to exact grams once starting weight is logged) |
|---|---|
| Protein | ~0.8–1.0 g per lb of bodyweight |
| Fat | ~0.3–0.35 g per lb of bodyweight |
| Carbs | Fills remaining calories after protein &amp; fat, adjusted by phase |
| Water | ~0.5–1 oz per lb of bodyweight, higher on lift days |

- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–6):** hold at maintenance while your trend weight gets established. This is baseline data, not a diet yet.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 7–12):** step into a real deficit, calculated off your actual Phase 1 average, not a guess made on Day 1.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 13–18):** hold the deficit, reassess weekly against your 160 lb target, adjust from real trend data.
- **Phase 4 (Weeks 19–24):** tighten the final stretch toward 160, then hold and stabilize once you're there.
- Protein at every meal, non-negotiable, this is the number that keeps your muscle while the fat comes off, and it's the "athletic physique" half of your goal, not just the scale number.
- Your sleep is sitting at 5 hours, pushing that toward 7–8 is going to help your recomp more directly than another set in the gym, low sleep raises appetite and blunts recovery both.

*General fitness and nutrition guidance from your coach, not a substitute for individualized clinical dietary advice.*
