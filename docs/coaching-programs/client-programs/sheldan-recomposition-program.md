## Weekly Structure

| Day | Focus | Why it's placed here |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps) | Fresh CNS for the heaviest pressing of the week, opens the split the same way your own PPL cycle already does. |
| **Day 2** | Pull (Back, Biceps, Rear Delts) | Full recovery from Day 1 pressing, balances the pressing volume so your shoulders stay healthy. |
| **Day 3** | Legs (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves) | The biggest compound day of the week, placed with a full recovery day on either side. |
| **Day 4** | Conditioning, Core &amp; Recomp Finisher | The piece your current split doesn't have, direct work toward the waist and body-fat side of recomp, not just the lifting side. |

On a 3-day week, skip Day 4 or fold the core circuit into Day 3's finisher. On a 4-day week, run all four as written.

## Day 1 / Push — Chest, Shoulders &amp; Triceps
*Heavy · ~50–60 min*

**Warm-Up**
- Band Pull-Aparts — 2 × 15
- Arm Circles — 2 × 10/direction
- Push-Up to Down Dog — 2 × 8
- Shoulder Dislocates (band or PVC) — 2 × 10

**Main Lifts**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load |
|---|---|---|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 × 6–8 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |
| Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 3 × 8–10 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 × 10–12 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |
| Cable Lateral Raise | 3 × 12–15 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |

Cues: Bench press, shoulder blades pinned, feet driving into the floor, bar path stays over the chest not the face. Shoulder press, ribs down, no lower-back arch to substitute for shoulder range. Lateral raise, lead with the elbow, stop at shoulder height, this is a shoulder isolation move not a momentum swing.

**Triceps Finisher**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load |
|---|---|---|
| Triceps Pushdown | 3 × 12–15 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |
| Overhead Rope Tricep Extension | 2 × 15–20 | Establish Week 1, log in Hub |

## Nutrition Protocol

Now about your eating: you told me the real gap already, dieting alone without much guidance on counting calories. That's not a discipline problem, that's a structure problem, so here's the structure. Exact gram targets lock in once you log your starting weight in the Hub Week 1, here's what Week 1 runs on.

| Macro | Target (locks to exact grams once starting weight is logged) |
|---|---|
| Protein | ~0.8–1.0 g per lb of bodyweight |
| Fat | ~0.3–0.35 g per lb of bodyweight |
| Carbs | Fills remaining calories after protein &amp; fat, adjusted by phase |
| Water | ~0.5–1 oz per lb of bodyweight, higher on Day 1 &amp; Day 3 |

- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4):** we hold at maintenance while I get four weeks of your real trend weight, no guessing at a deficit before I have real numbers.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8):** we step into a modest deficit, roughly 300–500 kcal under maintenance, calculated off your actual Phase 1 average, not a number I made up on Day 1.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12):** we hold the deficit, check in weekly against your 36-inch waist and sub-27% body fat target, and adjust off your real numbers.
- Protein at every meal, non-negotiable, that's the number that keeps your muscle while the fat comes off, and it's exactly what you said was missing last time.
- Your sleep is sitting at 6–7 hours, pushing that toward 8 is going to help your recomp more directly than another set in the gym, low sleep raises appetite and blunts recovery both.

*General fitness and nutrition guidance from your coach, not a substitute for individualized clinical dietary advice.*
