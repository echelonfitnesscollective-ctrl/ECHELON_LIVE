## Why I Built It This Way

**On the split:** you already built this one. The hybrid routine you sent over is almost exactly what we're running, I mostly formalized the periodization and merged it with a phase structure. When something works and it's clearly yours, my job is to sharpen it, not replace it.

**On "not a bodybuilder, not a powerlifter":** compound lifts stay first when you're freshest (Monday and Friday), accessory volume stays moderate instead of chasing size in one direction, and speed work, Zone 2, and a long run are layered in so the engine keeps pace with the strength. That's the "weak in the middle" problem solved, strength, size, and conditioning develop together instead of one eating into the others.

**On the knee:** a click with no pain usually points to a tracking or stability issue, not a structural one, but I'm not a physician, so get it looked at once before we push the plyometric work harder (see the note below). In the meantime, box squats replace free squats on Monday, controlling depth and shear force, Controlled Articular Rotations go into every warm-up, and single-leg and posterior-chain work builds the stability that usually resolves a painless click over time.

**On the "5% body fat" target:** I want to be straight with you on this one. 5% is stage-shredded, competition-only condition, essentially unsustainable, and it actively fights muscle gain since your body needs some fat reserve to build and recover. What actually gets you strong, muscular, and lean in a way you can hold onto sits closer to 8 to 12%: visible abs, visible separation, still enough in the tank to keep adding muscle and mileage. That's the number this program is actually built toward. If you want to chase single digits for a specific event down the line, we can run a short, planned cut for that, but not as the year-round target.

**On the calendar:** your own program already builds in a deload every 4th week. I kept that exactly as you designed it and used it as the engine for the whole periodization below, instead of imposing a different rhythm on top of it.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|

| **Phase 4: Polish & Performance Test** | 25–28 | Volume tapers ~15%. Week 28: retest the main lifts, the speed protocols, and the long run distance against Week 1 baseline, to see the actual gap closed. |

## Nutrition Protocol

Your goal is both "build muscle" and "get lean," and those pull in opposite directions at the extremes you named. Here's the honest split: we build first, then we cut, not both at once, that's how it actually works at an advanced level.

| Macro | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 1.0 g per lb of bodyweight/day |
| Fat | 0.35 g per lb of bodyweight/day |
| Carbs | Fills the rest of the target: higher in the Build phases, lower in the Cut phase |
| Calories | Maintenance + ~250–300 kcal in Build phases, Maintenance − ~350–400 kcal in the Cut phase |

- **Phases 1 & 2 (Weeks 1–16, Build):** modest surplus, carbs concentrated around lifting days (Mon/Wed/Fri). This is where the muscle gets built.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 17–24, Cut):** controlled deficit. Protein and training volume stay put so the muscle built in Phases 1–2 doesn't get eaten into, this is where the number on the scale and the mirror actually move toward that lean, defined look.
- **Phase 4 (Weeks 25–28, Maintain):** back to maintenance so you walk into the Week 28 retest recovered, not depleted.
- Exact gram targets lock in once we have your Week 1 morning weigh-in, log it in the Hub and I'll confirm the numbers the same day.
- Protein at every meal: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, lean beef, cottage cheese.
- Sleep is already solid at 7 hours, that's a real advantage most clients don't have. Protect it.

*General fitness and nutrition guidance from your coach, not a substitute for individualized clinical dietary advice. Get the knee looked at by a physician before we push the plyometric or box jump work to higher intensity, given the click you mentioned on the call.*

## Mobility & Knee Health: Frequency, Not Just a Day

A click with no pain is common and usually mechanical, not damage, but frequency of the right kind of work is what actually changes it, not one Saturday session.

- Knee CARs go in every single warm-up, all seven days, not just Saturday, that's the real fix.
- Box squat depth stays fixed at parallel or just above until the click is a non-issue for 4 straight weeks, then we can explore deeper ranges.
- Track how the knee feels weekly (fine / mild click / anything sharper) in the Hub; sharp or painful is the stop signal, mild painless clicking is not.
