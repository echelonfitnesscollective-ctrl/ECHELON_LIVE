# PERFORMANCE: WELCOME PACKET

**Tagline:** Your 12-week welcome packet, built for power, speed, agility, and real athletic output.
**Format:** 4 days a week: 2 power and speed-emphasis days, 2 strength-emphasis days, with dedicated warm-up and conditioning built into every session, not bolted on after.

---

## Welcome

Welcome to Echelon. You are training for something specific, a sport, an event, or general athleticism, and these 12 weeks are built around how fast and explosively you can move, not just how much you can lift. Strength is trained here as a means to power, not as the end goal.

## Your Program At a Glance

| Phase | Weeks | What's Happening |
|---|---|---|
| 1: Movement Prep | 1-3 | Landing mechanics, jump and sprint technique, base strength. |
| 2: Power Build | 4-7 | Explosive strength work, higher plyometric volume. |
| 3: Peak Power | 8-10 | Your highest power output, competition-specific work if it applies. |
| Taper | 11 | Volume cuts back, intent stays high, freshness is the priority. |
| Deload / Test | 12 | Retest your jump, sprint, and strength benchmarks. |

## How We Work Together

- Your day-by-day training lives in Today's Work inside your Member Portal, log in anytime to see what's next, and mark it complete once you finish.
- Check in honestly. Tell your coach what actually happened, not what you think we want to hear, an honest "I missed two days" helps us adjust; a false "everything's perfect" just delays the fix.
- If something hurts (not just the normal soreness of training), stop, message your coach before your next session, and see a clinician if it does not resolve. Never push through real pain.
- Your plan will change. What you start Week 1 with is not what you will be doing Week 8, that is not the plan failing, that is the plan working.

## What Makes Performance Different

Performance trains strength as a means to power, not as the end goal. If you are training for a sport, an event, or general athleticism, this program's plyometric work, sprint mechanics, and power-focused loading are built around how fast and explosively you can move, not just how much you can lift.

## The Echelon Standard

Every Echelon program is built on the same four things: Structure, a proven system that removes the guesswork; Accountability, coaching and check-ins that keep momentum moving; Commitment, progress earned by returning to the standard, especially when motivation is low; and Faith, strength that starts internally, character and purpose driving everything else. The exercises in front of you are specific to your goal. The standard behind them is the same for every person who trains with us.
