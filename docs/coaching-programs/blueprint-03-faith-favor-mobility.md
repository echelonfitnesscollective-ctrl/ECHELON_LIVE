# TRAINING CARD BLUEPRINT: FAITH & FAVOR MOBILITY

**Card copy (live on site):** "A restorative strength-and-mobility experience designed to improve how you move, recover, and feel between harder training days." Focus: mobility, core stability, posture, active recovery. 45-minute small-group session, all experience levels.
**Format type:** Recurring restorative class, the lowest-intensity card in the lineup by design; success here is measured in how a member feels and moves, not output.

---

## 1. Session Shape (45 min)

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Centering / Breathwork Opening | 5 min | Set tone, down-regulate from the day, establish breath pattern used throughout |
| Mobility Flow | 15–18 min | Joint-by-joint mobility sequence (ankles → hips → spine → shoulders) |
| Core Stability + Posture Work | 12–15 min | Anti-extension/anti-rotation core work, postural strength (upper back, glutes) |
| Restorative Cool-Down | 8–10 min | Longer static holds, breathwork close |

## 2. Weekly Rotation

Unlike Group Fitness's theme rotation, this format benefits from **consistency of structure with variety of specific movements:** members should always know the shape of the session (centering → mobility → stability → restore) even as the specific flow changes week to week to keep it fresh and address different joints/patterns.

| Week Focus (rotates) | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| Lower Body Mobility | Ankles, hips, hamstrings |
| Spine & Thoracic Mobility | Rotation, extension, posture |
| Shoulder & Upper Back | Overhead mobility, scapular control |
| Full-Body Integration | Ties the previous three weeks together |

## 3. All-Experience-Levels Design Principle

This card explicitly welcomes every fitness level, including members who find the other cards intimidating, design every movement with a floor-level entry (a chair or wall-supported version of anything getting down to the floor) so no one is excluded by a single exercise choice.

## 4. Live Coaching Cues & Modification Delivery

- Cue breath before and during every movement, this format's differentiator from a stretch-only class is the intentional breathwork pairing.
- Offer props (blocks, straps, chairs) proactively rather than waiting for someone to ask, normalizes using support as smart training, not a lesser version.
- "Restorative" does not mean "no coaching attention", still walk the room and correct posture/alignment, just at a calmer pace and volume than Group Fitness or VL Body Lab.
- Any sharp pain (versus a normal stretch sensation) is coached as "ease off, and see a clinician if it continues", this population often includes members managing existing injuries, so this distinction matters more here than anywhere else in the lineup.

## 5. Coach Notes

- This card is a strong complement/recovery day for members also doing 1-on-1 Coaching, 12-Week Transformation, or VL Body Lab, position it that way in conversation, not as a stand-alone "lesser" option.
- Track which members come consistently after harder training days versus as their only training, both are valid, but it's useful context for how to coach the room each week.
- Launch standard (per live site copy): opens once coaching coverage and a sustainable recurring schedule are confirmed, this blueprint is ready ahead of that launch decision.
