# ECHELON 12-WEEK BASE MODEL: PRENATAL

**Goal:** Train safely through pregnancy, protecting the pregnancy itself first and fitness outcomes second. Every exercise choice, position, and intensity target exists to stay inside what current guidance considers appropriate for a healthy, cleared pregnancy.
**Audience:** Clients who are currently pregnant, medically cleared to exercise by their provider before starting.
**Format:** 3 sessions a week (strength, core and pelvic floor, low-impact cardio and mobility), intensity self-regulated week to week.
**Status:** Base model, this is the one program that should change client to client and week to week more than any other. Personalize aggressively.

## 2. How This Program Actually Progresses

This program does not run on a fixed deload calendar, pregnancy is not a linear training block. Intensity is self-regulated week to week based on how the client actually feels: energy, symptoms, and her provider's guidance.

**Every client needs medical clearance to exercise before starting**, and should stop and contact her provider immediately for any warning sign: vaginal bleeding, dizziness, chest pain, calf pain or swelling, contractions, fluid leakage, or reduced fetal movement.

**General shape across the three trimesters:**
- **First trimester:** training looks close to her normal routine, adjusted mainly for nausea and fatigue, avoid overheating.
- **Second and third trimester:** avoid extended time flat on the back, avoid any exercise with real fall or abdominal-impact risk, favor the side-lying, seated, and standing variations built into this program, and let range of motion and load come down naturally as balance and comfort change.

Use the "talk test," she should always be able to hold a conversation. Every session should include the pelvic floor and diaphragmatic breathing work, that is the preparation for both delivery and postpartum recovery, not a throwaway warm-up. Reassess comfort and adjust at every single session.

## 6. What Sets This Program Apart

Prenatal is the only base program where the plan is expected to change every single session, not just phase to phase. Position, balance, and comfort shift throughout pregnancy in ways no template can predict in advance, so the coach's job here is continuous reassessment, not executing a fixed 12-week arc like the other six programs.
