# WEIGHT LOSS IN GROUP FITNESS

**Base program:** [Weight Loss: 12-Week Base Model](../../02-weight-loss-12-week.md)
**Format:** Recurring class · see [Group Fitness Blueprint](../../blueprint-01-group-fitness.md) for the general session shape

---

## How the base model translates here

Group Fitness can't hand every member an individualized 12-week plan, it runs on a published, repeating class schedule. What it *can* do is deliver the Weight Loss base model's Phase 1 training philosophy (full-body strength + metabolic conditioning, moderate intensity, sustainable pace) inside the existing 4-week class rotation, with live scaling replacing written personalization.

**Mapping the base model's split onto the rotation:**

| Weight Loss base model day | Group Fitness rotation slot |
|---|---|
| Full-Body Strength A/B/C | Week 1 (Full-Body Strength Circuit) and Week 3 (Strength + Core Focus) |
| Walk-based cardio | Not a class format, coached separately via Today's Work between sessions |
| Light circuit introduction | Week 2 (Metabolic Conditioning) |
| N/A | Week 4 (Mixed Format) doubles as the natural reassessment/community week |

A member on the Weight Loss track attending Group Fitness gets the *intensity and pacing philosophy* of the base model (RPE 5-7, form before load, walk-don't-run on conditioning) even though the specific exercises rotate with the published class theme, not a personal 12-week calendar.

## Personalization decision table

Group Fitness can't rewrite the room's programming per person, but intake data still drives real, individual decisions, mostly around scaling and safety, communicated live or via a quick pre-class note to the member.

| Intake field | What it changes in Group Fitness |
|---|---|
| **Training experience** | New/beginner clients get pointed to the floor-level scale-down on every exercise by default; advanced clients get the scale-up offered without being asked. |
| **Training days available** | If Group Fitness is their only structured training, recommend the walk-based cardio days from the base model as homework between classes, don't let group be their only 3-4 hours a week of activity if the base model calls for more frequency. |
| **Equipment access** | Not applicable, the room's equipment is fixed. |
| **Current activity level** | Sedentary/lightly-active clients start on the most conservative scale for their first 2-3 sessions regardless of what the room is doing that day, even if they seem eager to keep up. |
| **Injuries, conditions, pregnancy/postpartum** | This is the single most important field for Group Fitness, flag it to whichever coach is teaching that day *before* class, not during. Pregnancy modifications, joint issues, or a recent injury need a pre-briefed coach, not a surprised one mid-circuit. |
| **Medical clearance** | "Pending clearance" or "not yet discussed" means conservative scaling by default until resolved, don't wait for the member to self-advocate mid-class. |
| **Exercise preferences** | Minor lever here (room programming is fixed), but worth knowing, e.g., a client who hates burpees can be shown the low-impact swap proactively instead of grinding through resentfully. |
| **Sleep and stress** | A rough sleep/high-stress week is a cue to check in before class and suggest that day's scale-down is a smart choice, not a failure. |
| **Biggest consistency barrier** | If it's "time," Group Fitness's fixed schedule is either the solution (forces the show-up) or the obstacle (if the slot doesn't fit their life), worth a direct conversation about whether this is the right format at all. |

## Coach notes

- The intake profile turns a room of 12-15 people into 12-15 individually-considered members, even without individualized written plans, a 30-second glance at a member's profile before class (experience level, injuries, clearance status) is the whole point of collecting this data for a group format.
- If a Weight Loss client's profile shows heavy barriers (low activity, high stress, "not yet discussed" clearance, multiple injuries), Group Fitness alone is likely not enough structure, that's a signal to have the 1-on-1 or 12-Week Transformation conversation, not a reason to push them harder in the room.
