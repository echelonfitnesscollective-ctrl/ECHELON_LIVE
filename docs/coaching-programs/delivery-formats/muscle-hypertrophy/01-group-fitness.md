# MUSCLE (HYPERTROPHY) IN GROUP FITNESS

**Base program:** [Muscle (Hypertrophy): 12-Week Base Model](../../04-muscle-hypertrophy-12-week.md)
**Format:** Recurring class · see [Group Fitness Blueprint](../../blueprint-01-group-fitness.md) for the general session shape

---

## How the base model translates here

This is a real gap, and it's worth naming honestly: the Muscle (Hypertrophy) base model's edge is precision, targeted muscle-group volume tracked and progressed week over week, mind-muscle-connection coaching on specific exercises, and a periodization plan that deliberately climbs from ~10-14 sets per muscle group toward ~16-20 before deloading. None of that survives a room running a published, rotating 4-week class theme. Group Fitness cannot track one member's weekly chest volume against another's, and it cannot hold a single muscle group's progression steady across weeks the way the base model requires.

What Group Fitness *can* deliver is the base model's general training philosophy and its Phase 1 coaching cues, full-range compound and accessory movement, deliberate tempo, and "feel the muscle move the weight" cueing, inside the existing rotation. A member using Group Fitness as their hypertrophy training is getting a general strength-training stimulus with hypertrophy-style coaching cues layered on top, not the base model's actual volume progression.

**Mapping the base model's split onto the rotation:**

| Muscle (Hypertrophy) base model day | Group Fitness rotation slot |
|---|---|
| Push / Pull / Upper (mind-muscle-connection cueing) | Week 1 (Full-Body Strength Circuit) and Week 3 (Strength + Core Focus) |
| Legs (quad and posterior emphasis) | Partially covered in Week 1 and Week 3; leg-specific volume is the most under-delivered piece of the base model in this format |
| Metabolic-stress accessory work (higher reps, shorter rest) | Week 2 (Metabolic Conditioning), the closest natural match to Phase 1's rep ranges and rest periods |
| Intensity techniques (drop sets, rest-pause) | Not deliverable live in a group setting at meaningful scale, see Coach Notes |

## Personalization decision table

Group Fitness can't rewrite the room's programming per person or track one member's weekly set count, but intake data still shapes real, individual decisions, mostly around scaling, exercise substitution, and honest expectation-setting.

| Intake field | What it changes in Group Fitness |
|---|---|
| **Training experience** | New/beginner clients are steered toward the room's scale-down and coached on tempo and mind-muscle focus before load; advanced clients chasing real hypertrophy-specific progression are told plainly that this format alone won't deliver the base model's volume tracking, and are pointed toward 1-on-1 or 12-Week Transformation if that's their real priority. |
| **Training days available** | If Group Fitness is a hypertrophy-focused member's only structured training, set expectations that they'll see general strength and conditioning gains, not the base model's targeted muscle growth, recommend adding a second format if muscle growth is genuinely the priority. |
| **Equipment access** | Not applicable, the room's equipment is fixed. |
| **Current activity level** | Sedentary/lightly-active clients start on the most conservative scale for their first 2-3 sessions, same as any other goal track, before layering in mind-muscle-connection coaching. |
| **Injuries, conditions, pregnancy/postpartum** | Flag to whichever coach is teaching that day before class, the base model's pregnancy modifications (avoiding supine flat press, capping RDL/hip thrust load) need a pre-briefed coach in a room-based format, not one improvising mid-circuit. |
| **Medical clearance** | "Pending clearance" or "not yet discussed" means conservative load and scaling by default until resolved. |
| **Exercise preferences** | Minor lever given fixed room programming, but worth knowing, a client who dislikes a given movement can be shown the swap proactively rather than grinding through it half-heartedly, which matters more for hypertrophy coaching since disengagement undermines the mind-muscle-connection focus specifically. |
| **Sleep and stress** | A rough sleep/high-stress week is a cue to suggest that day's scale-down, high-volume hypertrophy-style training compounds recovery debt faster than the general fitness cueing in this room accounts for by default. |
| **Biggest consistency barrier** | If it's "time," Group Fitness's fixed schedule may be the whole solution for general fitness, but be honest that it's not a substitute for a hypertrophy-specific plan if targeted muscle growth is the actual goal. |

## Coach notes

- Set expectations honestly at intake: a member whose real goal is precision muscle growth (not just general strength and fitness) should be told this format delivers the training *philosophy*, not the base model's actual volume progression, and pointed toward 1-on-1 or 12-Week Transformation if that precision matters to them.
- Intensity techniques (drop sets, rest-pause) from the base model's Phase 3 are not practical to coach live across a full room safely and individually, skip them here rather than attempting a diluted version; they belong in a format with closer supervision.
- Group Fitness remains an excellent complement or on-ramp for a hypertrophy-minded member, general strength-building habits, movement competency, and consistency built here transfer directly once they're ready for a more individualized format.
