# PREGNANCY IN VL BODY LAB

**Base program:** [Preconception](../../07-preconception-12-week.md) · [Prenatal](../../08-prenatal-12-week.md) · [Postpartum](../../09-postpartum-12-week.md)
**Format:** High-energy athletic group class · see [VL Body Lab Blueprint](../../blueprint-04-vl-body-lab.md) for the general session shape

---

## How the base models translate here

VL Body Lab is built around plyometric, sprint, and high-impact power work. That is a genuinely good fit for one pregnancy stage and not appropriate at all for the other two, this format needs the clearest stage-based line of anywhere in the system.

**By stage:**
- **Preconception** is not pregnant yet, and this base model is explicitly written to train at full intensity with no built-in limits. VL Body Lab is a legitimate, even ideal, delivery format for a Preconception client who wants more intensity than Group Fitness offers, there is no pregnancy-specific reason to hold her back here.
- **Prenatal is not an appropriate fit for this format at any trimester.** Plyometric loading, sprint work, and high fall-risk movements sit directly against the Prenatal base model's core guidance (avoid abdominal-impact and high fall-risk exercise). Redirect to Group Fitness, 1-on-1, or Faith & Favor Mobility instead.
- **Postpartum is not appropriate until well into Phase 3**, and only with a pelvic floor physical therapist's clearance specifically for running, jumping, and heavy lifting, the same threshold the base model sets for reintroducing that work at all. Even then, ease in, don't return directly to a full-intensity class.

## Personalization decision table

| Intake field | What it changes in VL Body Lab |
|---|---|
| **Pregnancy stage** | The hard gate for this entire format. Preconception: full participation is appropriate. Prenatal: redirect, do not enroll. Postpartum: redirect until Phase 3 is well underway with pelvic floor PT clearance. |
| **Currently pregnant (any trimester)** | This alone is sufficient reason to redirect away from VL Body Lab, regardless of how fit or experienced the client was before pregnancy. |
| **Postpartum phase** | Phase 1 or 2: redirect, no exceptions. Early Phase 3 without PT clearance: still redirect, the base model's own threshold for running/jumping/heavy lifting has not been met yet. |
| **Pelvic floor PT clearance (Postpartum)** | The specific, individual signal that a Postpartum client may be ready to reintroduce this format, general "feeling back to normal" is not the same thing and should not be treated as equivalent. |
| **Training experience (Preconception)** | Same as any other goal in this format, new/beginner clients default to the built-in regressions on the power/speed block regardless of pregnancy status. |
| **Sleep and stress (Preconception)** | Same logic as any goal, poor sleep or high stress is a cue to scale the power block down that day. |

## Coach notes

- Treat any pregnancy flag as an automatic redirect conversation for this format specifically, don't let a client's own confidence or prior fitness level override the stage-based gate.
- If a Prenatal or early Postpartum client specifically wants VL Body Lab's intensity, that desire is worth naming as a goal for later, once she's Preconception again or well into cleared Postpartum Phase 3, not something to accommodate now with a heavily modified version of a fundamentally high-impact class.
- Preconception clients coming from VL Body Lab who then move into Prenatal are a natural, expected transition out of this format, position it that way rather than as a loss of intensity.
