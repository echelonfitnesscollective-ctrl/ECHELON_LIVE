# ECHELON 12-WEEK BASE MODEL — BULKING

**Goal:** General mass gain — maximize total tissue growth (muscle plus some fat) with a simpler, higher-volume approach than the hypertrophy-refined Muscle program below. Bulking is the "eat more, lift heavy, grow" model; Muscle is the "precision hypertrophy" model for a client already carrying good muscle who wants refined growth without much fat gain.
**Audience:** Intermediate+ clients in a caloric surplus, training base required, cleared through intake.
**Format:** 4-day upper/lower split, higher volume than Cutting, minimal conditioning work (recovery capacity is reserved for lifting).
**Status:** Base model — personalize load and exercise selection per client.

---

## 1. Weekly Split

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Upper Body A (Push-emphasis) |
| 2 | Lower Body A (Quad-emphasis) |
| 3 | Rest or light mobility |
| 4 | Upper Body B (Pull-emphasis) |
| 5 | Lower Body B (Posterior-emphasis) |
| 6 | Optional arms/weak-point accessory day |
| 7 | Rest |

---

## 2. 12-Week Periodization

| Phase | Weeks | Rep Range | Rest | Intensity (RPE) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Base Volume | 1–4 | 8–12 | 90s | 6–7 | Establish working weights, build volume tolerance |
| 2 — Load | 5–8 | 6–10 | 90–120s | 7–8 | Progressive overload on compounds, volume holds |
| 3 — Peak | 9–11 | 5–8 | 120s | 8–9 | Heaviest loading block, compounds prioritized |
| Deload | 12 | 10–12 | 90s | 5–6 | Volume cut ~40%, reassess strength numbers |

**Progression rule (linear load):** add load every 1–2 weeks on the primary compound of each day as long as all reps are completed with good form. This is a more straightforward load progression than Cutting's density method, appropriate because recovery resources are being spent on growth, not simultaneously on a deficit.

---

## 3. Day 1 — Upper Body A (Push-Emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 x 6–10 | 120s | Chest, Triceps |
| Overhead Press | 4 x 6–10 | 90–120s | Shoulders, Triceps |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 x 10–12 | 90s | Upper Chest |
| Lateral Raise | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Side Delts |
| Dips (or bench dips) | 3 x 8–12 | 60s | Triceps, Chest |
| Rope Tricep Pushdown | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Triceps |

**Form cues:** Full ROM on bench and overhead press — bulking volume is wasted if range of motion is clipped for ego load.
**Coaching cues:** "Load the muscle, not the ego" — form breakdown to chase a number is the fastest way to stall a bulking block with an injury.
**Modification down:** Machine chest press and machine shoulder press if joint stress from free-weight pressing is a limiter; landmine press as a shoulder-friendlier overhead alternative.
**Modification (pregnancy):** Bulking is not an appropriate goal to assign during pregnancy — redirect to the Weight Loss or a maintenance-focused personalized plan and flag for a nutrition conversation outside this program's scope.

---

## 4. Day 2 — Lower Body A (Quad-Emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back Squat | 4 x 6–10 | 120s | Quads, Glutes |
| Leg Press | 4 x 10–12 | 90s | Quads, Glutes |
| Walking Lunge | 3 x 10/leg | 60s | Quads, Glutes |
| Leg Extension | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Quads |
| Standing Calf Raise | 4 x 12–15 | 30–45s | Calves |

**Modification down:** Front-foot-elevated split squat instead of back squat if a client lacks squat mobility; leg press as the primary quad driver instead.

---

## 5. Day 4 — Upper Body B (Pull-Emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted Pull-Up (or Lat Pulldown) | 4 x 6–10 | 120s | Lats, Biceps |
| Barbell or Chest-Supported Row | 4 x 8–10 | 90s | Back |
| Single-Arm Dumbbell Row | 3 x 10–12/side | 60s | Lats |
| Face Pull | 3 x 15 | 45s | Rear Delts |
| Barbell or Dumbbell Curl | 3 x 10–12 | 45s | Biceps |
| Hammer Curl | 2 x 12–15 | 30s | Biceps, Forearms |

**Modification down:** Lat pulldown at a manageable assist level instead of weighted pull-up until pulling strength is built; band-assisted rows if grip is a limiter.

---

## 6. Day 5 — Lower Body B (Posterior-Emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deadlift (conventional or trap-bar) | 4 x 5–8 | 150s | Posterior Chain |
| Hip Thrust | 4 x 8–10 | 90s | Glutes |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 8–10 | 90s | Hamstrings, Glutes |
| Seated Leg Curl | 3 x 12–15 | 45s | Hamstrings |
| Weighted Hanging Leg Raise | 3 x 10–12 | 45s | Core |

**Form cues:** Deadlift — bar over mid-foot, brace before the pull, hips and shoulders rise together.
**Modification down:** Trap-bar deadlift or rack pull from a comfortable pin height while hinge mechanics are being built.

---

## 7. Day 6 — Optional Weak-Point Accessory Day

Coach-selected based on the individual — this is the one day in the base model explicitly built to be personalized rather than templated. Typical use: arms for a client who wants more size there, calves/rear delts for stubborn body parts, or an extra light technique session on a compound lift that needs work.

---

## 8. Coach Notes

- **Weekly check-in:** weigh-in trend (target: 0.25–0.5% bodyweight gain per week — faster than that is mostly fat, flag it), strength log on primary compounds, sleep, energy, joint feel.
- **Bulking is a controlled surplus, not "eat everything"** — coach nutrition conversations toward a moderate surplus so the mass gained stays predominantly muscle over the 12 weeks.
- **Personalization starting points:** volume tolerance varies widely — some clients grow on this exact template, others need volume trimmed to recover between sessions. Watch for accumulating joint pain from the higher weekly tonnage and back off before it becomes an injury.
- Pain during any exercise is coached as "stop, and see a clinician" — never worked through or diagnosed on the spot.
