THE ECHELON STANDARD

BUILD A BODY. BUILD A STANDARD.

Echelon isn't built on motivation. It's built on an expectation: how you show up, how you carry yourself, how you keep the promises you make to yourself. That doesn't move. Your goal, your stage, your starting point, that's what gets built around it.

Motivation can start a change. It rarely finishes one. What lasts is showing up the same way on the good days and the bad ones, whether you're just starting, coming back from a setback, or already performing at your best.

That's the whole idea behind Echelon: it doesn't change based on where you are. A beginner gets the same commitment as an advanced athlete. Someone rebuilding gets the same respect as someone at their peak.

MEET THE COACH

COACHING WITH A STANDARD.

ISSA CERTIFIED PERSONAL TRAINER

I've been around sports for as long as I can remember. I never made it to the professional level, but I had the privilege of competing alongside some of the best athletes of my generation.

Whether I was the new guy, coming back from an injury, on the bench, or starting, one thing never changed: there was always a standard, an expectation for how you showed up and what you brought to the people around you.

That's what I coach now. Not just a workout. The discipline, accountability, and systems that hold you to your best, at whatever stage you're in.
